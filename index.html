          Where to get real Afro sounds
          
🎙 Record your own. Shakers, bottles, a table, hand claps, your voice. Free, unique, and 100% yours.

          
💳 Paid sample libraries (like Splice): huge Afrobeats sections, cleared for use in your songs.

          
🆓 Free loop and sample sites (like Looperman or Freesound): great sounds, but every upload has its own license. Some require credit, some forbid commercial use.

          
🎁 Producer drum kits: many Afro producers give away or sell kits. Read the terms that come with them.

          
⚖️ Ownership lesson: if you don't clear a sample, you don't fully own your song, and you can't sell its royalties or build a token on it. Keep a note of where every sound came from.
