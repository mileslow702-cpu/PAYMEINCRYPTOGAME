🤖 Your project plan + AI agent

Your whole plan, phase by phase: the project bible, idea scorecard, budget, schedule, checklists, funding and release. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Grok and it becomes your own producer: it asks where you are, runs the play step by step, and redoes the math when you change a number.

How to set it up
  1. Claude: new Project → paste into the project instructions.
  2. ChatGPT: new Project (or Explore GPTs → Create) → paste into instructions.
  3. Grok: new Project → paste into the instructions, or paste it as the first message of a chat.
  4. Then start with: "I'm in pre-production. Here's what's done: …"
